# Phase 0: AI-Driven Leasing Acquisition System

Date: April 26, 2026

## Purpose

Phase 0 expands OmniLease from lead response into lead creation. The current
product answers and converts inbound demand. Phase 0 creates more qualified
demand for each property through short-form content, paid distribution,
AI-assisted DM response, review monitoring, and conversion tracking.

Mission: fill units faster with a measurable acquisition system.

## Positioning

OmniLease Lead Response remains the core automation product. Phase 0 is the
managed acquisition layer that sits in front of it:

1. Create content that generates renter interest.
2. Distribute that content through organic posting and paid ads.
3. Capture inbound messages and form fills.
4. Respond in under 60 seconds with AI-assisted leasing conversations.
5. Qualify prospects, book tours, and follow up until a lease decision.
6. Track the funnel from spend to lead, tour, lease, and vacancy-day reduction.

If Phase 0 cannot track conversion and vacancy impact, the model does not work.

## Client-Side Requirements

Each property must provide the following before launch:

- Unit access for filming.
- Pricing and availability data updated daily or weekly.
- Floor plans, amenities, fees, policies, and move-in requirements.
- Leasing contact, escalation owner, and CRM access when available.
- Approval for ad spend, recommended at `$500-$2,000/month` minimum.
- Google Business Profile access or manager-level review alert access.
- Approval rules for public review responses and complaint escalation.

## Core System

### Content And Distribution

- ChatGPT or OmniLease prompt workflows for scripts, hooks, offers, and creative
  variations.
- CapCut or equivalent editing workflow for captions, cuts, and templates.
- Meta Platforms Ads for paid distribution.
- Organic posting workflow for short-form property content.
- Creative library per property with hooks, captions, status, and performance.

### Lead Capture And Automation

- GoHighLevel or OmniLease CRM as the first lead store.
- AI DM responder for Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and website chat where
  channel access is available.
- SMS and email follow-up automation when consent and channel policy allow it.
- Calendar booking via Calendly, Google Calendar, or OmniLease scheduling.
- Human escalation for complaints, fair housing, pricing negotiation, legal,
  urgent, or low-confidence conversations.

### Review And Alert Management

- Google review monitoring for new reviews, rating drops, and keyword alerts.
- AI-assisted response drafts for approved review categories.
- Immediate human alerts for 1-2 star reviews, safety issues, legal threats,
  discrimination claims, fraud claims, unresolved maintenance complaints, and
  staff-name complaints.
- Owner-visible review status: new, drafted, approved, posted, escalated, closed.
- Weekly reputation report with average rating, review volume, unresolved
  negative reviews, recurring themes, and response-time SLA.

## Operational Workflow

1. Onboard property data, CRM access, review access, and ad budget.
2. Film the property in one `2-4 hour` batch.
3. Generate `20-40` short-form video variations.
4. Publish organic content and launch paid ad campaigns.
5. AI handles inbound DMs, website chats, and approved lead forms.
6. AI qualifies leads, captures contact info, and books or requests tours.
7. Follow-up automation continues until tour booked, lease decision, opt-out, or
   human takeover.
8. Google reviews and alerts are monitored daily.
9. Weekly reporting ties spend, leads, tours, leases, reviews, and vacancy impact
   back to property-level outcomes.

## Mandatory Performance Tracking

Phase 0 must track:

- Cost per lead.
- Cost per tour.
- Cost per signed lease.
- Lead-to-tour conversion rate.
- Tour-to-lease conversion rate.
- Response time, with target under `60 seconds`.
- Qualified leads per month.
- Additional leases per month.
- Vacancy days reduced.
- Ad spend, campaign status, creative performance, and source attribution.
- Review response time, negative-review resolution time, and unresolved alerts.

Target outcomes:

- `20-40` qualified leads/month per property.
- `+2` to `+5` additional leases/month per property.
- Response time under `60 seconds`.
- Reduced vacancy time per available unit.

## Pricing Models

### Model 1: Base Plus Performance

Recommended default.

- Base: `$2,000-$4,000/month` per property.
- Performance: `$50-$150` per qualified lead, or `$300-$800` per signed lease.

Why: aligns incentives and is easier for owners to evaluate against leasing
outcomes.

### Model 2: Flat Retainer

- `$2,500-$5,000/month`.
- Includes `12-20` videos/month, ad management, AI lead capture, basic review
  alerts, and reporting.

Downside: caps upside and does not directly tie pricing to signed leases.

### Model 3: Performance Heavy

Use only when OmniLease controls the full funnel.

- Base: `$500-$1,000/month`.
- Performance: `10-20%` of first month's rent per signed lease.

Example: if rent is `$1,800`, OmniLease earns `$180-$360` per lease.

## Phase 0 Done Definition

Phase 0 is ready to sell when one pilot property can run the full loop for at
least one reporting period:

- Content is published.
- Ads are live or organic distribution is active.
- Leads are captured with source attribution.
- AI responds to inbound messages and escalates sensitive threads.
- Tours or tour requests are tracked.
- Review alerts are monitored and routed.
- Weekly report shows cost per lead, cost per tour, cost per lease when
  available, conversion rates, and vacancy impact.
